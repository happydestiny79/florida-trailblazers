FLORIDA TRAILBLAZERS: Case 1, The Stolen Founding Stone (v0.1 playable)

HOW TO PLAY
  Double-click index.html. It opens in any modern browser (Chrome/Chromebook, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
  No internet, no install, no accounts. Progress saves automatically in the browser.
  You can also serve the folder (for example: python3 -m http.server) and open http://localhost:8000/

FOR TEACHERS
  Click "Teacher view" on the title screen. It lists each Florida benchmark, its official 2026 wording,
  and the scenes that teach it, plus an answer key.
  FACT_CHECK.md lists every historical claim with its source.

CHANGE THE TITLE
  Edit GAME_TITLE in js/config.js.

All art is drawn with code (SVG). The Stone, thief, suspects and guides are made up. The history is real.

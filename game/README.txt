FLORIDA TRAILBLAZERS: Case 1, The Stolen Founding Stone (version 6.0 preview)

HOW TO PLAY
  Double-click index.html. It opens in any modern browser (Chrome/Chromebook, Edge, Firefox, Safari),
  on a computer, tablet or phone. No internet, no install, no accounts. Progress saves automatically
  in the browser. You can also serve the folder (for example: python3 -m http.server) and open
  http://localhost:8000/
  Sound effects are made live by the browser (no audio files) and start OFF; use the speaker button.
  Read-aloud uses the device's built-in voice (no audio files). Tap a 🔊 button next to a passage,
  clue, question or answer, or turn on "Read to me" so each new screen is read automatically.

WHAT'S NEW IN V6 PREVIEW
  - Read-aloud for students who struggle with reading. A speaker button sits on story text,
    reading passages, clues, questions, answer choices and Local Flavor text. Words highlight
    as they are read (or the whole block highlights if the device cannot track words).
  - "Read to me" can be turned on per student (title screen, in-game Menu, or Teacher view).
    When it is on, each new screen reads the story and then the question. Speed: Slow or Normal.
  - Tapping a vocabulary word still opens the meaning, and also reads the word and definition.
  - Uses only the browser's built-in voices (Web Speech API). No downloads and no extra cost.
    Voice quality depends on the device. Some phones need one tap before the first auto-read.

WHAT'S NEW IN V5 PREVIEW
  - Painted kid-detective portraits (24 original detectives, 3 expressions each) replace the
    SVG avatar builder. Pick a detective, try an expression, and choose a painted Florida
    backdrop for your ID card. Earned badges appear on the Detective ID card (not as hats
    pasted on the portrait). Old saved looks migrate to a matching painted detective.
  - In-game Detective ID card (portrait, name, rank, case number, badges, and a stamp when
    a case is finished). Open it from My Detective or the win screen.

WHAT'S NEW IN V4 PREVIEW
  - Local Flavor arrival postcards at all 7 real map places (22 verified-license photographs).
  - Swipeable place albums; tappable vocabulary; on-tap photo attribution and photo credits.
  - Rotating witness clues tied to the real chase destinations.
  - Optional Spot it: +2 exploration points once per stop, without changing the case score.
  - Preserves the temporary teacher Report button: append ?debug to the page URL.
  - Preview only: see LOCAL_FLAVOR_NOTES.md for scope, factual sources and review instructions.

WHAT'S NEW IN V3
  - Portraits with 5 expressions for every character (painted portraits: original AI-generated art of made-up people).
    Tap a character to interview them: the questions you pick decide which clues you earn, and
    rude accusations end the interview.
  - Suspect dossiers (wanted posters) that fill in one clue at a time, and an Arrest Warrant
    (suspect + hideout + evidence). A wrong warrant lets the suspect escape; Sam helps you fix it.
  - Hands-on clue scenes: tap-to-search hotspots with a magnifying glass, a torn map to rebuild,
    and dust to wipe off the Founding Stone. Touch, mouse and keyboard all work.
  - Penny Pinch leaves riddle notes tied to the standards and shows where she escaped on the map.
    Finish early for a speed bonus. Teachers can switch on Relaxed mode (no timer, no race bonus).
  - Class detective looks and ranks save on this device. (v5: painted portraits and an ID card; badges still unlock from case work.)
  - Class features with no server: class list (first names/nicknames), weekly leaderboard,
    team mode (2-3 partners take turns), and share codes to merge scores from several devices.
    No accounts, no network calls; student data never leaves the device.

WHAT'S NEW IN V2
  - Real photos, historic images and NASA/USGS maps (all public domain or Creative Commons; see
    CREDITS.md or the Credits button). Tap the camera caption on any scene for the photo's credit.
  - Detective Sam, a partner who helps with questions (never answers) in 3 steps.
  - Badges and an end-of-case detective rank.
  - Reading passages tagged with Florida B.E.S.T. ELA benchmarks.
  - New question types: drag-to-order timelines (touch, mouse or the up/down buttons), matching,
    typed short answers, choose-the-evidence and pick-all-that-apply. Chapters get harder.
  - Vocabulary words: tap (or hover) a purple underlined word for a meaning and picture. Words
    collect in the Notebook's Glossary tab.

FOR TEACHERS
  Click "Teacher view" on the title screen: where students got stuck (tries, hints, wrong answers),
  results by student, CSV export, Social Studies and ELA benchmarks with official wording and the
  scenes that teach them, and an answer key. Records stay in this browser only.
  Under Class settings you can turn on "Read to me" as a default for new students, or set it
  (and the reading speed) for each student on the roster.
  FACT_CHECK.md lists every historical claim with its source. CREDITS.md lists every image.
  Some content (slavery and freedom at Fort Mose, the 1704 end of Mission San Luis) deserves a preview.

CHANGE THE TITLE
  Edit GAME_TITLE in js/config.js.

The Stone, thief, suspects and guides are made up (all characters are fictional; portraits are original AI-generated art, not real people). The history is real.

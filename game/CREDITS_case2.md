## Highsmith / Library of Congress

Carol M. Highsmith photographs of the Tampa Bay Hotel, the Tampa skyline, and the Sunshine Skyway come from the Carol M. Highsmith Archive at the Library of Congress. Highsmith has dedicated these photographs to the public domain ('no known restrictions on publication').

The 1835 Fort Brooke picture *Tampa Bay on the Gulf of Mexico* is a historic lithograph (Gray & James) from the Library of Congress, public domain.

The Florida manatee photograph is a U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service work (Steve Hillebrand), public domain as U.S. government work.

## Accuracy notes that shape the story

- **Weedon Island is not the Tocobaga capital.** Pinellas County and archaeological sources place the Tocobaga main town / type site at Philippe Park in Safety Harbor. Weedon Island has earlier shell middens and a burial mound associated with a long Native occupation (and with pottery later called Weeden Island). Kid-facing text keeps this distinction.
- The **exact landing beaches** of Narváez (1528) and de Soto (1539) on Tampa Bay are debated. Kid-facing text says "the Tampa Bay area."
- The Tampa Bay Hotel's **exact length** is reported differently (NPS: 900 feet; Plant Museum: 1/4 mile). Kid-facing text does not pick one length.
